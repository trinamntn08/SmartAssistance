Before you send text

Generate sends the captured writing field and rewrite settings to the SmartAssistance API and OpenAI after consent. Surrounding page content is not sent. Reading translation uses Chrome's local models under a separate notice.

The app does not save draft history. Captured text is temporary and expires after ten minutes. It is cleared when you recapture, navigate, close the source tab, or choose Clear text and consent.

OpenAI retention depends on the provider account and model settings. Disabling response storage does not guarantee zero provider retention.
